02 · How we work

Can't find it? Our sourcing desk will.

Shortages, long lead times and end-of-life notices are part of the job. Haoyu Chin runs our sourcing and works through our supplier network to find the parts your distributor has run out of.

Step 1

Send your list

Email part numbers, quantities or a full BOM to sales or sourcing. A spreadsheet is fine.

Step 2

Get a clear quote

We come back with pricing and availability per line, plus alternatives where a part is hard to get.

Step 3

Parts on the way

Once you confirm, we ship from the UK and keep you updated until the parts arrive.
